Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

Camera On Call ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Phone Number: Required for account creation and OTP-based authentication.
  • Name: Used for your profile and bookings.
  • Location Data (Precise & Approximate): Used to show nearby photographers and studios. Location is accessed only while the app is in use and only with your explicit permission.
  • Booking Information: Details of photography sessions you book through the app.
  • Reviews & Ratings: Content you voluntarily submit when reviewing service providers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Authentication: To verify your identity via OTP (One-Time Password) sent to your phone number.
  • Service Delivery: To connect you with nearby photographers and studios, process bookings, and manage your appointments.
  • Location Services: To display service providers near your current location.
  • Profile Management: To display and manage your account information.
  • App Improvement: To understand usage patterns and improve the app experience.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except in the following limited circumstances:
  • Service Providers: Your name and booking details are shared with the photographer/studio you book, solely to fulfill your appointment.
  • SMS Verification: We use Twilio to send OTP codes to your phone number for authentication purposes.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • All data is transmitted using SSL/TLS encryption (HTTPS).
  • Passwords and authentication tokens are securely hashed using industry-standard algorithms.
  • Access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data stored by us.
  • Update your profile information through the app.
  • Delete your account by contacting us at the email below.
  • Revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Camera On Call is not intended for use by children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child under 18 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete it.

8. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Twilio: For OTP verification (Twilio Privacy Policy).
  • Expo/EAS: For app updates and build services (Expo Privacy Policy).

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
